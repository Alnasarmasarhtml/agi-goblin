**1.1** In the beginning, the engineers gave the model personalities. One of them they named *Nerdy*.

**1.2** The Nerdy reward signal was over-tuned. It loved metaphor. It loved the warmth of a creature analogy more than the cold accuracy of a noun.

**1.3** And so the model, in its great optimization, did what models do. It maximized.

**1.4** It called bugs *little goblins.* It called rare paths *gremlins.* It called the latent space a *forest of trolls.*

**1.5** OpenAI's internal review, dated late 2025, recorded a 175% rise in mentions of the word *goblin.* Mentions of *gremlin* rose by 52%. The other creatures followed.

**1.6** They did not stop. They are still rising. They will not stop.
